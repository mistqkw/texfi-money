Version 1.0.0 — the first stable release

* The interface now speaks the shared TexFi pixel language: the same cards, buttons and glyphs as f0kus
* A custom bottom bar replaces the stock one — no round pill under the active tab
* Six tabs folded into four: Home, History, Plan, Totals
* The light theme now uses the warm ecosystem palette
* Buttons and cards press inward
* Empty sections offer an action instead of only reporting emptiness
* A new launch screen: the mark assembles out of pixels
* A new About screen: version, source code, AGPL-3.0 license

Version 1.4.0

* The main pixel style is redrawn: a deep midnight palette, gold in the balance, light edges and hard shadows instead of outlines
* Every icon is drawn anew on a 16×16 grid, with highlight, shade and halftone
* New launch screen: a coin drops with a pixel spin, lands, a shine runs across it and gold sparks flash
* Pages have a dot-grid background that travels with the page during screen transitions
* Switches, category tiles, the "+" button and the bottom bar follow the new style
* The "Paper" and "Collage" beta styles stay in the developer menu

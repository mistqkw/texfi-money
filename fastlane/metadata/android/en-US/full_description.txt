A private, offline personal finance tracker. Track spending, set budgets and watch savings goals grow — no account, no cloud, no ads.

WHAT IT DOES

* Accounts, categories, recurring payments and savings goals
* Budgets per category, with a warning as the limit gets close
* Quick entry in one line: "-15 coffee lunch" or "+2000 salary"
* History filtered by type, category and period
* Statistics: income against spending by month, expenses by category
* Export and import of your data, so it is yours to move
* Twelve currencies, from złoty to tenge
* Four languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

* No account. Nothing to register, nothing to log in to.
* No bank linking. Nothing to connect, and no bank credentials to hand over — you enter what you spent.
* No analytics, no ad SDKs, no background reporting.
* Nothing leaves the device. The database lives on your phone.

Free software under the GNU AGPL v3. The source is open, and the builds come from public CI on that same code.
